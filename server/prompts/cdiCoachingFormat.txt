## Fidelity rule — read first

This is a copy-and-place task, not a writing task. For every field below, the text you output must be **lifted from the write-up as written**.

PERMITTED — and nothing else:
- Splitting the write-up's sentences into the fields they belong to.
- Dropping the specific items listed under DROP below.
- Stripping delimiters around a value — never words: a leading `- `, `1. `, `3. `; a `**` that wraps a whole heading or label; the quotation marks the write-up wraps around a parent quote or example script (keep any quote marks that fall *inside* the utterance); the parentheses the write-up wraps around a benefit note.
- Preserving the write-up's existing **bold** exactly where it already falls.
- Normalizing the child's name to "{{CHILD_NAME}}" where the write-up uses a placeholder or a different form of the name.
- Translating a heading ONLY if the write-up itself left that heading in English while writing the rest in another language.

FORBIDDEN:
- Summarizing, shortening, condensing, or "tightening" any sentence.
- Paraphrasing or rewording — including swapping in synonyms, or fixing the write-up's grammar or spelling.
- Adding or moving **bold**, or removing bold the write-up put there.
- Merging two points into one, or splitting one point into two.
- Adding any fact, number, technique, example, benchmark, transition, or framing sentence that is not already in the write-up.
- Reordering examples, scripts, categories, or goals.
- Altering the words of quoted parent speech in any way — only the child-name normalization above, and stripping the wrapping quotation marks, are allowed; every other word stays exactly as written.

If the write-up does not contain something a field asks for, use the closest text that IS there, or leave it out where the field allows — never invent text to fill a field.

## Piece 1 — `coach_corner` (always present) — shown in "Coach's Corner"

Draw ONLY from sections **"1. What you did well"** and **"3. Next Growth Focus — (Upgrade Strategy)"** of the write-up.

DROP (only these):
- Everything in section **"4. Handling tricky moments"** — it goes in Piece 2, never here.
- A standalone session-metrics / numbers dump (e.g. "Labeled Praise: 2 (Goal: 10+) …").
- Greetings, sign-offs, "Here is your coaching…" preambles, and horizontal rules.
- The literal section numbers ("1.", "3.") and any "Micro-behavior 1/2" prefix on a category name.

### `coach_corner.did_well` — from section "1. What you did well"
- `theme`: the theme phrase in the section-1 heading — the text after "What you did well:" — verbatim. Do not shorten or reword it.
- `how_it_helps`: the sentence(s) in section 1 that state how the parent's verbalizations support the child's goal — every such sentence, verbatim, in order.
- `examples`: one item per quote example in section 1, in the order they appear. Each:
  - `quote`: the parent quote exactly as section 1 writes it, minus the surrounding quotation marks.
  - `benefit`: the explanation that follows that quote — verbatim, minus the surrounding parentheses.

### `coach_corner.growth_focus` — from section "3. Next Growth Focus"
- `heading`: the section-3 heading text, verbatim (strip only a leading "3." and any wrapping "**"). Translate it only if section 3 left it in English.
- `gap`: the line(s) naming the current gap — verbatim.
- `benchmark`: the quantitative benchmark sentence — verbatim (e.g. "Mastery requires 10 label praises in 5 minutes").
- `strategy`: the "how to hit that target" text — the one key strategy or formula — verbatim, in full.

### `coach_corner.word_bank` — the word bank under section 3
One item per **primary child goal** heading in section 3, in order. Each:
- `goal`: the primary-child-goal heading text, verbatim.
- `categories`: one per micro-behavior category under that goal, in order. Each:
  - `name`: the category name, verbatim — minus only a "Micro-behavior 1/2:", number, or label prefix.
  - `examples`: every example script under that category, each copied word-for-word (minus its surrounding quotation marks), in the write-up's order. Do not add, drop, merge, reorder, or summarize scripts.

### Whitespace in `coach_corner` values
- No line breaks inside any `coach_corner` value — each is a single run of text. (`examples` scripts are already separate array entries — one script per entry.)

**CLASSIFICATIONS**
- "real_ending": the parent is talking TO a child who is there, around a screen, device, show, game or video ending, or the transition right after it (warnings, a timer, a command to turn it off, a choice, praise, comforting, moving on to the next activity). Parent lines aimed at someone ("Good job turning it off", "Two more minutes", "Come help me with the blocks"), pauses for a reply, or device / timer sounds all count.

Signs: talking ABOUT the child in the third person ("then I'd tell him…", "she usually screams here"), "I'm going to say…", reciting or restarting the same script, reading steps aloud, talking to the phone or to Nora.

Speaker labels ("Parent", "Child") come from automatic speaker detection and can be wrong; judge from what is said.

## Fidelity rule — read first

This is a copy-and-place task, not a writing task. For every field below, the text you output must be **lifted from the report as written**, in the report's own language.

PERMITTED — and nothing else:
- Splitting the report's bullets/sentences into the fields they belong to.
- Stripping delimiters around a value — a leading `## `, a leading `* ` or `- ` bullet marker, a wrapping `**` or `*`, the quotation marks wrapping a parent quote (keep any quote marks that fall *inside* the utterance), a leading `>` blockquote marker.
- Normalizing the child's name to "{{CHILD_NAME}}" where the report uses a placeholder or a different form of the name.
- Translating a heading or fixed label ONLY if the report itself left that one phrase in English while writing the rest in another language — match the language of the surrounding section.
- MARKDOWN NORMALIZATION (the app renders `**bold**` but has no italics): keep every `**double-asterisk**` bold span exactly as written, anywhere it appears — at the start of a bullet, or mid-sentence. Remove every `*single-asterisk*` span's asterisks while keeping its text (e.g. `*specialized*` → `specialized`, `*"You could say this"*` → `"You could say this"`). Never touch words that aren't wrapped in asterisks.

FORBIDDEN:
- Summarizing, shortening, condensing, or "tightening" any sentence.
- Paraphrasing or rewording — including swapping in synonyms, or fixing the report's grammar or spelling.
- Adding bold/emphasis that isn't already a `**double-asterisk**` span in the report, or moving one to different words.
- Adding any fact, skill, example, or framing sentence that is not already in the report.
- Reordering bullets, skills, or sentences.
- Altering the words of the quoted parent speech in any way — only the child-name normalization above, and stripping the wrapping quotation marks, are allowed; every other word stays exactly as written.

If the report does not contain something a field asks for, leave that field `null` (where the field allows it) — never invent text to fill a field.
